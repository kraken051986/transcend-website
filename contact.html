Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you help with business setup?

Business setup timelines vary by jurisdiction and business type. Mainland companies typically take 2-3 weeks, while free zone setups can be completed in 1-2 weeks. We'll provide a specific timeline during your consultation.

Do you offer free initial consultations?

Yes, we offer a complimentary 15-minute consultation to understand your business needs and recommend the best solutions for your specific requirements.

What information do you need to get started?

For most services, we'll need basic information about your business, including business activity, expected turnover, number of employees, and your specific requirements. We'll guide you through exactly what's needed.

Can you help with businesses outside Dubai?

Yes, we provide services across all UAE emirates and can assist with business setup and compliance throughout the country.
